Frequently Asked Questions

Does this option include Technical (core engineering) teaching?

No. This option covers only the Non-Technical / Rajasthan GK portion - 100 hours of live classroom teaching in Rajasthan History, Geography, Art & Culture, and the Political & Administrative System. It does not include core Technical subject teaching. If you also want live Technical classes, see the Technical + Test Series or Complete Course options on the शौर्य Batch comparison page.

Does this option include the Test Series?

No. This is classroom teaching only - no test series is bundled with this option. If you would like the RSSB JE Diploma Test Series bundled with your Non-Technical classes, see the Non-Technical + Test Series option instead.

How much of the exam does the Non-Technical portion cover?

Non-Technical / GK is worth 40 of the 120 total marks in the RSSB JE exam, as per the official exam scheme - and this weightage is the same for both the Degree and Diploma papers.

Where are the classes held?

At our offline centre in Jaipur - 33, White House, Opp. Zone Tech, Tonk Road, Madhuvan Colony, Mansingh Pura, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302015. This is currently the only city for शौर्य Batch.

When does the batch start, and what are the class timings?

Classes are tentatively starting in September 2026. Exact timings will be announced to enrolled learners in advance.

Do I get an admit card or ID with this option?

Yes. Since this option has no bundled test series, you get your own separate roll number and admit card, used just for classroom attendance at the Jaipur centre.

Is recorded content provided?

No, शौर्य Batch is a live offline classroom program - there are no recorded lectures. Subject-wise DPPs and formula sheets for the Non-Technical portion are shared as downloadable PDFs to support your self-study.

Is there a seat limit?

Yes - शौर्य Batch is capped at 200 seats in total, shared across all six शौर्य Batch options, not 200 seats per option. Enrollment closes once the batch is full.

Is there a refund policy?

Please message us on WhatsApp before enrolling if you have any concerns about fit. Once enrolled and the batch has started, refunds are not applicable.

How do I enroll?

Click Enroll Now and complete the payment. The program will appear inside your Enrolled Programs section on your dashboard, and you will receive your admit card and further batch details. For any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.
