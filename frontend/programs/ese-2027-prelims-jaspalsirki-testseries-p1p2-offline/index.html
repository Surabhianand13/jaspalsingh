Frequently Asked Questions

Is this series for Paper 1, Paper 2, or both?
This series covers both Paper 1 (General Studies & Engineering Aptitude) and Paper 2 (Civil Engineering). Both papers are conducted on the same Sunday each week, exactly like the actual ESE Prelims exam day.
Which cities is this offline series available in?
This offline series is available at centres in Jaipur and Delhi only. After enrollment, you will receive centre details and timing on your registered number.
What is the paper pattern for the weekly tests?
Weekly tests (Tests 01-09): Paper 1 - 50 questions / 100 marks / 60 minutes; Paper 2 Civil - 75 questions / 150 marks / 90 minutes. Full-length tests (Tests 10-13): Paper 1 - 100 questions / 200 marks / 2 hours; Paper 2 Civil - 150 questions / 300 marks / 3 hours. Negative marking: 1/3 mark per wrong answer.
What happens if I miss a test?
If you miss a test, you will still receive the question paper and Detailed Solution Booklet for self-practice. However, missed tests cannot be rescheduled as they are conducted at a fixed time at the offline centre.
Is the fee refundable?
No refunds once enrollment is confirmed. We encourage you to review the schedule carefully before enrolling. For any queries, WhatsApp us at +91 98291 33317.
Can I switch from this offline series to the Printed OMR series?
No, once enrolled, switching between series is not allowed. Please choose the series that best fits your preparation style - offline (centre-based) or Printed OMR (home-based).
