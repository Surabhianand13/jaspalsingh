Frequently Asked Questions

Which exam is this test series for?

This series is for UPPSC's U.P. Technical Education (Teaching) Service Examination-2025 (Advertisement No. A-11/E-1/2025), specifically for the post of Lecturer, Government Polytechnics, Civil Engineering branch.

What is the paper pattern for this test series?

Paper I: Hindi (25 questions, 75 marks) + Civil-I (100 questions, 300 marks) - total 125 questions / 375 marks, duration 2 hours 30 minutes. Paper II: General Studies (25 questions, 75 marks) + Civil-II (100 questions, 300 marks) - total 125 questions / 375 marks, duration 2 hours 30 minutes. Every test date in this series includes both papers.

What is the test schedule?

12 tests across 6 fixed dates - 16 Aug, 20 Aug, 23 Aug, 27 Aug, 30 Aug and 3 Sep 2026 - with Paper I and Paper II conducted on each of these dates.

How does the Printed OMR Offline format work?

On each scheduled date, you receive the Paper I and Paper II question papers (PDF) along with blank OMR sheets by email. Attempt both papers at home within the given time, fill the OMR sheets, and submit clear photos or scans via email before end of day.

What is the fee for this test series?

The price is Rs 599 (MRP Rs 1,499) - Save 60%. No coupon code needed, this is the final checkout price.

Is there a refund policy?

Please message us on WhatsApp before enrolling if you have any concerns. Once enrolled and the batch has started, refunds are not applicable. We recommend messaging us first to confirm this program suits your preparation stage.

How do I enroll?

Click Enroll Now and complete the payment. The program will appear inside your Enrolled Programs section on your dashboard. You will then receive the Paper I and Paper II question paper PDFs by email on each scheduled test date. For any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.

What is the difference between this test series and a full course?

This is a test series, not a full syllabus teaching course. It gives you exam-pattern practice through 12 tests across 6 fixed dates, along with detailed solutions and performance tracking - it does not include topic-wise lectures or study notes.

Is this suitable for aspirants who are not near a physical test centre?

Yes. This program is fully printed OMR offline - there is no offline centre for this test series. Aspirants from anywhere can enroll and attempt every test from home.

Do I need to print anything?

Yes. You will need to print (or use on a screen as reference) the question paper PDFs and fill a standard OMR sheet on paper for both Paper I and Paper II. Then take a clear photo or scan of each completed OMR sheet and submit it via email before end of day on the test date.
