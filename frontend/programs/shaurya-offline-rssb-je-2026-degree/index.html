Frequently Asked Questions

How much does शौर्य Offline Test Series cost?

₹1,600 in Jaipur, Delhi or Alwar, and ₹1,999 at any other Rajasthan centre. Click Enroll Now and you will select your centre on the checkout page - the price updates automatically before you pay.

Why is the price different in different cities?

Jaipur, Delhi and Alwar are priced lower (₹1,600) since they are our primary hub centres. All other Rajasthan centres are a flat ₹1,999, regardless of whether you're attempting the Degree or Diploma series.

Is this the same as the existing RSSB JE Degree Offline Test Series?

No. शौर्य Offline Test Series is a separate, newly launched test series with its own start date (30 August 2026) and complete 24-test schedule. The existing RSSB JE Degree Offline Test Series continues to run separately, unaffected, for learners already enrolled in it.

How many tests are there and what's the pattern?

24 tests in total - 9 subject-wise tests (Tests 01-09) followed by 15 full-length mock papers (Tests 10-24), each with 120 questions. Tests run weekly from 30 August 2026 to 7 February 2027.

Do I get a printed solution booklet or a PDF?

A Detailed Solution PDF is provided after every test - not a printed booklet. This helps you analyse mistakes, identify weak areas, and improve for upcoming tests and the actual exam.

Which cities is this test series available in?

शौर्य Offline Test Series is conducted Pan Rajasthan and in Delhi. Pricing is ₹1,600 in Jaipur, Delhi and Alwar, and ₹1,999 at any other Rajasthan centre - select your centre at checkout for the exact price and to enroll.

What is the difference between the Degree and Diploma शौर्य Offline Test Series?

The Diploma series (22 tests) is for candidates with a Diploma in Civil Engineering applying under the Diploma category. The Degree series (24 tests) is for B.Tech / B.E. Civil Engineering graduates applying under the Degree category, with a deeper technical syllabus. Choose based on your qualification and the category you are applying under for RSSB JE 2026.

Where can I get the official RSSB JE 2026 notification?

The complete official RSSB JE 2026 notification (Advt. 10/2026) is available as a free download on this page - see the "Free Download" section above. It covers full eligibility, reservation categories and the application process.

What happens after I pay the enrollment fee?

After payment, you will receive a confirmation email and a details form to fill in - once reviewed, your roll number and admit card become available for download from your profile. If you have any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.

Is there a refund policy?

Please contact us on WhatsApp before enrolling if you have concerns about eligibility or suitability. Once enrolled and the series has started, refunds are not applicable.
