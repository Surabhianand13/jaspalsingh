Frequently Asked Questions

What is RVUNL JE 2026?

RVUNL JE 2026 refers to the Common Recruitment of Junior Engineer-I in the State Power Companies of Rajasthan (Advertisement No. RVUN/Rectt.-2026-27/02, notified 4 Aug 2026). It covers Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam (RVUN, generation), Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut Prasaran Nigam (RVPN, transmission) and three distribution companies - JVVN, AVVN and JdVVN.

Which companies does RVUNL JE 2026 cover?

The recruitment is common across five Rajasthan state power companies - RVUN (generation), RVPN (transmission), and the three distribution companies JVVN, AVVN and JdVVN. A single JE-I selection can lead to posting in any of these.
