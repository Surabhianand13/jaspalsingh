Frequently Asked Questions

Does this option include Non-Technical/GK teaching?

No. This option covers only the 500-hour live Technical classroom programme (Part B of the RSSB JE 2026 exam). It is built for candidates who are already handling Rajasthan GK/Non-Technical (Part A) preparation on their own. If you also want live Non-Technical teaching, see the Complete Course (Diploma) or Non-Technical + Test Series options on the शौर्य Batch comparison page.

What part of the RSSB JE 2026 exam does this classroom teaching cover?

RSSB JE 2026 (Combined Recruitment) has two parts totalling 120 marks in a 2-hour objective MCQ paper: Part A - General Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Geography, Culture & Polity, 40 marks) and Part B - Technical, subject-specific (80 marks). There is 1/3rd negative marking per wrong answer and a 40% minimum qualifying requirement. This programme's live classroom teaching covers Part B (Technical) only.

Which Test Series is bundled with this option?

The full RSSB JE Diploma Test Series is bundled in - the same product our test-series-only learners use. It is auto-linked to your account at purchase, so you don't need to enroll separately.

Is this option for Degree or Diploma candidates?

This page is for Diploma (Civil, Mechanical or Electrical) candidates preparing for RSSB Junior Engineer 2026. If you are a Degree (Civil) candidate, see the Technical + Test Series (Degree) option instead.

Where are the classes held?

At our offline centre in Jaipur - 33, White House, Opp. Zone Tech, Tonk Road, Madhuvan Colony, Mansingh Pura, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302015. This is currently the only city for शौर्य Batch.

When does the batch start?

Tentatively September 2026. Class timings will be announced to enrolled learners in advance.

Is there a seat limit?

Yes - शौर्य Batch is capped at 200 seats in total, shared across all 6 शौर्य Batch options, to keep classroom sizes manageable. Enrollment closes once the batch is full.

Is recorded content provided?

No, शौर्य Batch is a live offline classroom programme - there are no recorded lectures. Subject-wise DPPs and formula sheets are shared as downloadable PDFs to support your self-study.

Do I get an admit card / ID?

Yes - the same roll number and admit card used for your bundled Test Series doubles as your classroom attendance ID, so you only need to carry one.

What is the fee for this option?

The price is Rs 12,999 (MRP Rs 29,999) - Save 57%.

Click Enroll Now and complete the payment. The programme will appear inside your Enrolled Programs section on your dashboard, along with your linked Test Series enrollment. For any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.
