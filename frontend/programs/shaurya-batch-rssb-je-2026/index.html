Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the 6 शौर्य Batch options?

The 6 options combine three building blocks - live Technical classroom teaching (500 hrs for Degree, 350 hrs for Diploma), live Non-Technical classroom teaching (100 hrs), and the bundled RSSB JE Test Series - in different mixes, and at Degree or Diploma level. Complete Degree and Complete Diploma include everything for their respective level. Technical + Test Series and Non-Technical + Test Series include a subset plus testing. Non-Technical Only includes just the classroom teaching, with no bundled test series.

Where are the classes held?

At our offline centre in Jaipur - 33, White House, Opp. Zone Tech, Tonk Road, Madhuvan Colony, Mansingh Pura, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302015. This is currently the only city for शौर्य Batch.

When does the batch start?

Tentatively September 2026. Class timings will be announced to enrolled learners in advance.

Is there a seat limit?

Yes - शौर्य Batch is capped at 200 seats across all 6 options combined, to keep classroom sizes manageable. Enrollment closes once the batch is full.

Is recorded content provided?

No, शौर्य Batch is a live offline classroom program - there are no recorded lectures. Subject-wise DPPs and formula sheets are shared as downloadable PDFs to support your offline preparation.

Do I get an admit card or ID?

Yes - you'll get a roll number and admit card for शौर्य Batch itself once your details form is reviewed and approved, which also serves as your classroom attendance ID.

What is the RSSB JE 2026 exam pattern?

RSSB JE 2026 (Junior Engineer) is an objective MCQ exam of 120 marks over 2 hours - Part A: General Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Geography, Culture and Polity, 40 marks) and Part B: subject-specific Technical questions (80 marks). There is 1/3rd negative marking and a 40% minimum qualifying requirement. It covers Civil (Degree), Civil (Diploma) and Mechanical/Electrical (Diploma) posts.

How do I choose the right option for me?

If you want everything in one place, pick Complete Degree or Complete Diploma based on your qualification level. If you are already confident in one part of the syllabus, pick Technical + Test Series or Non-Technical + Test Series to save on cost. If you only need structured GK teaching, Non-Technical Only is available on its own. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317 if you would like help deciding.
