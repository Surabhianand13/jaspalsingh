Frequently Asked Questions

How much does this test series cost?

₹599 flat, for every learner regardless of location - no centre selection needed since you attempt from anywhere. MRP is ₹1,999.

How many tests are there and what is the schedule?

15 full-length tests from 15 November 2026 to 1 February 2027 - weekly on Sundays, plus extra tests on 21 Jan, 28 Jan and 1 Feb 2027. See the full schedule above for every date. Each test has 120 questions and follows the RSSB JE 2026 Civil Diploma exam pattern.

Yes. This series is specifically for Civil Diploma candidates. The question papers are tailored to the RSSB JE 2026 Diploma syllabus. If you are a Degree candidate, please enrol in the Degree version of this series.
