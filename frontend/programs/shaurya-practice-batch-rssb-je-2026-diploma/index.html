Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is included in शौर्य Offline Practice Batch Course (Diploma)?

10,000+ quality questions across Technical (Civil Engineering, Diploma level) and Non-Technical (Rajasthan GK) subjects, discussed live at our Jaipur centre by Dr. Jaspal Singh and Lekhwani Sir.

Where are the sessions held?

At our offline centre in Jaipur - 124/172, Agarwal Farm, Mansarovar, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302020. This is currently the only city for this batch.

When does the batch start?

The new batch (Batch 2) starts 17 November 2026. Session timings are shared with enrolled learners on WhatsApp and email.

Is there a seat limit?

Yes - this batch is open to the first 100 learners only, to keep it small and personal. Enrollment closes once the batch is full.

Is recorded content provided?

No, this is a live offline batch - there are no recorded lectures.

Do I get an admit card or ID?

Yes - you'll get a roll number and admit card once your details form is reviewed and approved, which serves as your classroom attendance ID.

How do I enroll?

Click Enroll Now and complete the payment. You'll receive a confirmation email and a details form to fill in - once reviewed, your roll number and admit card become available on your dashboard. For any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.

Is there a refund policy?

No. All fees are non-refundable once paid. Please contact us on WhatsApp before enrolling if you have any questions about eligibility or suitability, so we can confirm this program is the right fit for you.
