Frequently Asked Questions

How much does this UKPSC AE Civil test series cost?

₹1,999 flat, for every learner regardless of location - there is no centre selection since you attempt from home. Click Enroll Now to pay and get started.

How many tests are there and when do they start?

155 tests in total across 31 weeks - 105 subject-wise tests (Tests 01-105, 3 October 2026 - 21 February 2027) followed by 50 full-length mock tests (Tests 106-155, "FLT 1" through "FLT 10", 27 February - 2 May 2027). Every weekend has 5 tests: three Saturday shifts (Non-Technical) and two Sunday shifts (Civil Engineering papers).

What is the Saturday test pattern?

Every Saturday has 3 shifts: Shift 1 - Hindi or English (25Q each), Shift 2 - General Studies and Aptitude (25Q), Shift 3 - Uttarakhand Studies (25Q). In Phase 2 full-length tests, each Saturday shift has 100 questions.

What is the Sunday test pattern?

Every Sunday has 2 shifts: Shift 1 covers Civil Engineering Paper 1 subjects (50Q), Shift 2 covers Civil Engineering Paper 2 subjects (50Q). In Phase 2, Sunday tests are complete full-length papers of 180 questions each.

Do I need to print anything?

Yes. On every test day you will receive the question paper PDF and a blank OMR sheet by email - print both, attempt the paper within the given time, fill your answers on the OMR sheet with a blue or black ballpoint pen, then reply to the test email with a clear photo of your completed sheet before the end-of-day deadline.

What happens after I pay the enrollment fee?

After payment, you will receive a confirmation email - our team will personally reach out to you on WhatsApp or email to collect your details and get you started. If you have any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.

Is there a refund policy?
