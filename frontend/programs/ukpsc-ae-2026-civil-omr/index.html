About This Test Series

UKPSC AE (Assistant Engineer) Civil is conducted by the Uttarakhand Public Service Commission for Civil Engineering posts in Uttarakhand government departments. This 155-test series by Dr. Jaspal Singh covers every subject in the UKPSC AE syllabus - both the Non-Technical paper (Hindi, English, General Studies, Uttarakhand Studies) and the Civil Engineering Paper 1 and Paper 2.

All tests use real printed OMR sheets - the same format as the actual UKPSC AE exam. You give each test at home under timed conditions: receive the question paper and blank OMR by email on test day, fill and upload your OMR, and receive the solutions PDF and your score within 24 hours.

Phase 1 (21 weeks) covers subjects in a structured week-by-week sequence. Phase 2 (10 weeks) consists of full-length mock tests - exactly as in the actual exam.
