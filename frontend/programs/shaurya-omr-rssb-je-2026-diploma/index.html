Frequently Asked Questions

How much does शौर्य Printed OMR Offline Test Series cost?

₹999 flat, for every learner regardless of location - there's no centre selection since you attempt from home. Click Enroll Now to pay and get started.

What is the difference between this and शौर्य Offline Test Series?

Same schedule, same question papers, same 22 tests - the only difference is how you attempt them. शौर्य Offline is attempted in person at a Jaipur/Delhi/Alwar/other Rajasthan centre; this Printed OMR variant is attempted at home using a printed question paper and OMR sheet emailed to you, then submitted back for evaluation alongside the offline participants.

Do I need to print anything?

Yes. On every test day you'll receive the question paper PDF and a blank OMR sheet by email - print both, attempt the paper within the given time, fill your answers on the OMR sheet with a blue or black ballpoint pen, then reply to the test email with a clear photo of your completed sheet before the end-of-day deadline.

How many tests are there and what's the pattern?

22 tests in total - 7 subject-wise tests (Tests 01-07) followed by 15 full-length mock papers (Tests 08-22), each with 120 questions. Tests run weekly from 30 August 2026 to 24 January 2027 - identical to the शौर्य Offline schedule.

Will I be evaluated and ranked with offline test-takers?

Yes. Your results are evaluated and ranked together with शौर्य Offline Test Series participants, on the exact same question paper, so you know exactly where you stand.

Do I get a printed solution booklet or a PDF?

A Detailed Solution PDF is provided after every test - not a printed booklet. This helps you analyse mistakes, identify weak areas, and improve for upcoming tests and the actual exam.

Where can I get the official RSSB JE 2026 notification?

The complete official RSSB JE 2026 notification (Advt. 10/2026) is available as a free download on this page - see the "Free Download" section above. It covers full eligibility, reservation categories and the application process.

What happens after I pay the enrollment fee?

After payment, you will receive a confirmation email and a details form to fill in - once reviewed, your roll number and admit card become available for download from your profile. If you have any queries, message us on WhatsApp at +91 98291 33317.

Is there a refund policy?

Please contact us on WhatsApp before enrolling if you have concerns about eligibility or suitability. Once enrolled and the series has started, refunds are not applicable.
