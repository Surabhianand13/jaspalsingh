Frequently Asked Questions

Which post and advertisement is this test series for?

This test series is for the post of Assistant Public Sanitary & Waste Management Officer under the Nagar Vikas evam Awas Vibhag, as per BPSC Advertisement No. 108/2025.

How many vacancies are there for BPSC Bihar Sanitary Officer 2026?

There are 54 vacancies for the Assistant Public Sanitary & Waste Management Officer post as per BPSC Advt. 108/2025.

Who is eligible to apply / take this test series?

Candidates with a Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Environmental Science, Civil Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Public Health Engineering or Bio Technology are eligible, as are candidates with a Bachelor of Engineering in Chemical, Civil, Environmental Science, Public Health Engineering or Bio Technology, or a degree in Planning or Architecture. Please verify exact eligibility against the official BPSC Advt. 108/2025 notification.

What is the exam pattern for BPSC Bihar Sanitary Officer 2026?

Both papers are compulsory and objective (MCQ) type, held on the same day. Paper I - General Studies has 125 questions for 100 marks in 2 hours. Paper II - Solid & Liquid Waste Management has 125 questions for 100 marks in 2 hours.

How many tests are included in this test series?

20 tests in total across 10 test dates - on each date you attempt both Paper I (General Studies) and Paper II (Solid & Liquid Waste Management), exactly like the real BPSC exam day. 5 dates cover subject-wise practice, followed by 5 dates of full-length mocks for both papers.

What is the difference between the Offline and Home-Based OMR test series?

The Offline test series (this page) is conducted pen-and-paper at physical centres in Patna and Delhi. We also offer a Home-Based OMR Test Series for candidates who cannot attend a centre - you get the same question papers, attempt from home, and are evaluated together with offline participants at a lower price point.

When does the batch start?

The batch is tentatively scheduled to begin in September 2026. Exact test dates will be shared with enrolled students closer to the start date and once the official BPSC exam date is announced.
