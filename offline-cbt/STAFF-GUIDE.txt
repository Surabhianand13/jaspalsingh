OFFLINE CBT - STAFF GUIDE
Dr. Jaspal Singh - jaspalsingh.in

BEFORE THE EXAM DAY
--------------------
1. Open data.js in a text editor and replace ROSTER (the learner list
   for this slot) and EXAM (the real question bank) - it currently has
   sample/placeholder data only.
2. Change STAFF_PIN in app.js away from the default (1947).
3. Copy the whole "offline-cbt" folder onto a USB drive.

SETTING UP EACH COMPUTER
--------------------------
1. Copy the "offline-cbt" folder from the USB onto that computer
   (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click "launch-exam.bat" inside that folder.
   - This opens the exam fullscreen, with no address bar and no way
     to switch to another window or tab - learners can't browse
     anywhere else or accidentally close it.
3. To close it (between learners, or at end of day for syncing):
   press Alt+F4. This is a Windows shortcut, not a browser one, so
   it always works even in the locked-down kiosk view. Then just
   double-click launch-exam.bat again for the next learner.

DURING THE EXAM
-----------------
- Learner enters their mobile number, confirms their name, starts
  the test, answers questions, and submits.
- The score is calculated and saved on that computer, but is NOT
  shown to the learner - they just see "results announced separately."

SYNCING RESULTS (no need to close the app for this)
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect the computer to the staff hotspot.
2. On the exam screen, click the bottom-right corner of the screen
   5 times quickly. It will ask for the staff PIN.
3. Enter the PIN - this opens the Sync panel, showing how many
   results on this machine are still pending.
4. Click "Sync Now." Safe to click more than once - already-synced
   results are never sent twice.
5. Close the panel and disconnect from the hotspot to free it up for
   the next machine.

If a sync fails partway (hotspot drops etc.), nothing is lost -
just reconnect and click Sync Now again.
